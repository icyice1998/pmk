How cases are matched to TOS topics

Each topic of the Table of Specification has a keyword list (shown in Sources as "TOS 2568-2569 topics"). A case is counted under every topic whose keywords appear in its diagnosis, for example "Thyroid storm with AF with RF type 1" counts under Hyper/Hypothyroidism, Arrhythmia and Acute respiratory failure. Topics that an inpatient diagnosis cannot show, such as PTF interpretation, tobacco counselling, ICD10, genetics and research methods, are left out. Diagnoses that match no topic are counted as Unclassified.
